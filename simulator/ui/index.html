Pool & in-flight requests

Closed-loop load: most of the pool is reading or thinking at any moment, so in-flight sits well below pool size. The gap between the two lines is the workload's duty cycle.

Time to first token · rolling p50 / p95

Computed over the trailing turns of the measuring window. Rising p95 with a flat p50 is the first sign of queueing.

Time per output token · rolling p50 / p95

Decode pacing per token. Degrades when batches widen past the engine's comfort point.

Engine & host utilization

KV cache is the engine's own gauge; CPU is the bound-set (pinned cores only); GPU is SM utilization averaged across devices. Absent lines mean that collector isn't available on this host.

Token rates · prefill vs decode

From the engine's own counters. Prefill (prompt ingest) is compute-bound; decode (generation) is memory-bandwidth-bound — spiky prefill with sagging decode is the query-storm signature.

Session states

Where every session in the pool is right now: in prefill, streaming (decode), warm think (mid-conversation — their KV is the prefix cache's hot set), or cold (fresh sessions, nothing cached).
