Capacity numbers are only as good as the engine config they were measured on. Sweep the curated registry, or run the guided search over the whole parameter space, then benchmark on the winner.

Engine optimizer

Sweeps engine launch shapes (KV pool, batch width, chunked prefill, TP vs data-parallel replicas) against representative latency/throughput cells and ranks them — run this on a new host before the first capacity benchmark, then encode the winner in the profile you benchmark with. Results persist to runs/engine_optimizer/ and resume if interrupted.

Guided search samples the space for coverage, ranks candidates by the space's objective (default: summed throughput with p95-latency caps), then refines one-dimension neighbors of the leaders until the budget is spent or improvement flattens. Dimensions, objective and budget live in the space YAML (config/search/).
